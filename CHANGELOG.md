## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- Verdict banner with recovery checklist on failure (restic-style)
- Distinct exit codes: 0 clean, 2 transport/auth failure, 3 integrity failure, 4 usage/budget
- `--json` machine-readable summary, `--max-duration N` budget, `--dry-run` connectivity check, `--metadata-only` vs `--deep`, `--quiet`
